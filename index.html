Process

How I work

  1. 01

    Brief

    You send the footage, references, and the goal of the video, and we agree on length, style, and deadline.

  2. 02

    First cut

    I build the story first: the hook, the structure, and the pacing, before any polish goes on.

  3. 03

    Review

    You get a preview link to comment on, and I revise until it’s right.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Final files exported in the sizes and formats each platform needs, ready to post.
